## Env files: hard rule

**Never read `.env`, `.env.local`, `.env.development`, `.env.production`, `.env.test` or any other `.env*` file, in `client/` or `server/`, through any tool.** The only exception is `server/.env.example`. The client's `.env.*.example` files are off limits too (the user's global rule allows only `.env.example`).

- Running code that loads env values counts as reading them. A user-level hook blocks shell commands containing the text `.env`, which includes `process.env` and `import.meta.env`. Search for env usage with the Grep tool instead (e.g. pattern `env\.[A-Z_]+` or `VITE_[A-Z_]+`).
- If a value is needed, tell the user which variable to set.
- `npm run env:check` in `server/` lists missing keys by name only.

## What this is

My Planet View: people keep a private travel map of the world. They mark the countries they've visited, upload trip photos (matched to landmarks), plan trips, and share maps or trips Google Docs style. The design decisions and their reasons are in `docs/SITE-DESIGN.md`; the client–server contract for the travel features is `docs/API.md`. Keep both in step with the code.

## The travel model (`server/prisma/schema.prisma`)

- **`TravelMap`**: one owner. It has a `groupType` (`SOLO`, `COUPLE`, `FAMILY`), a `linkAccess` (`RESTRICTED` or `ANYONE_WITH_LINK`) and a unique `shareCode`.
  - Every user gets a default map ("My map", `isDefault`) from a better-auth hook at sign-up. There's one default per user, enforced by the database: `defaultForOwnerId` (the owner's id on the default map, null elsewhere) is unique, and a CHECK (`travel_map_default_owner`) keeps it in step with `isDefault`. Set or clear both together; `ensureDefaultMap` uses the winner when two requests race (P2002).
  - The default map can't be deleted.
- **`Traveller`**: the people a map is for (no login needed; `userId` when linked), with birth and favourite countries (a country or Antarctica, never a pole). A new or changed birth country marks it `VISITED` on the map (`markBirthplaces` in `controllers/maps.js`, `createMissingVisits`; 17 Nov 2026, the user's wish: a new account's map starts with its birthplace), on `POST /maps` and the travellers routes, which need no verified email; the mock does the same. One traveller per account per map (`@@unique([mapId, userId])`). A map's kind limits its travellers (17 Nov 2026, the user's wish: a couple's map is two people): `SOLO` one, `COUPLE` two, `FAMILY` up to 20 (`GROUP_MAX_TRAVELLERS` in `utils/travelValidation.js`; the client's `GROUP_TYPES` in `utils/map/saveMapSetup.js`, the mock's `handlers/maps.js`): adding past it is a 409, and so is changing to a kind with room for fewer than the map has. The set-up form shows "Add another traveller" only while there's room, and saves removals before the kind changes; a birth moment offers to add the baby only then (`MapExperience`); Settings' "Saved" is a green rubber stamp (`components/common/SavedStamp.jsx`), kept by the panel as the form starts afresh after a save. Each adult has their own login (19 Nov 2026, the user's decision: never a shared one) and the people a map is about are its co-owners (below); a co-owner's traveller is linked to their account. An optional **date of birth** (`birthDate`, 23 Nov 2026, the user's wish: asked when people are added to a map, never at sign-up; migration `20261123220000_traveller_birth_date`, CHECK `traveller_birth_date`; `parseBirthDate` in `utils/travelValidation.js`, 1900 to today): editors only (the map reply's travellers beside `userId`), for the stats' `byTraveller[].lifeAwayShare` (editors only too: `statsFrom` `role`, `buildMapStats` `showBirthDates`). The client asks it in Settings' set-up form (`MapSetupForm` `askBirthDate`, `TravellerFieldset`), never on `/welcome`; a birth moment's baby gets the moment's day; `utils/map/birthDates.js`. The privacy policy says so (1.3.1).
- **Who has access:**
  - **`MapMember`** and **`TripMember`**: people added as `VIEWER` or `EDITOR`, or on a map `CO_OWNER`. The owner is never a member row.
  - **Co-owners** (19 Nov 2026, the user's wish; docs/API.md "Co-owners", migrations `20261119120000_co_owner_role` and `20261119120100_co_owner_invites`): one of the map's travellers with their own login, with every power the owner has on that map but deleting it, transferring it, making it the main map, paying for it and taking a co-owner's role away (`canManage` is owner or co-owner, `isOwner` the owner alone, `canSetMemberRole`/`canRemoveMember` in `access.js`). Adding one names the traveller (`travellerId`; a pending invite carries it, `ShareInvite.travellerId`, CHECK `share_invite_co_owner`), linked on joining (`linkTravellerInTx`, `acceptInviteInDB`), let go on leaving (`deleteMapMember`); at most one fewer than the travellers (`checkCoOwnerCap`), and their travellers can't be deleted (`deleteTravellerInDB`). A co-owner is the default heir before an editor (`heirFor`); a transfer to a co-owner keeps the old owner as one. **Signing up from an invite's link** sends its `inviteToken`: an account with the invite's own address is made verified (`verifiedByInvite`, better-auth's user `create.before`; its verification email skipped) and its invites accepted at once. `GET /api/invites/:token` tells the invite page what it's for; the sign-up form keeps its address and goes straight to the map (`RegisterForm`, `JoinedMapNote`). Client: `utils/sharing/shareRoles.js` (`canManageRole`, `coOwnerChoices`), the share dialog's "Which traveller is this?"; the mock mirrors it (`handlers/sharing.js` `acceptInviteFor`); `tests/integration/coOwners.test.js`, `sharing.spec.js` "Co-owners".
  - **`ShareInvite`**: an emailed invite for an address with no verified, active account. The token is stored hashed, and a CHECK constraint in the init migration makes it target exactly one of a map or a trip. It turns into a member when that address signs up and is verified.
- **`CountryVisit`** (map × country): `VISITED`, `LIVED` or `WANT_TO_GO`, with a `source`: `MANUAL`, `PHOTO` or `TRIP`. `notes` is the country's one note ("I want to visit here"): text or null, cleaned, cut to `NOTES_MAX`.
- **`TripMap`** (trip × map, `addedById`, `addedAt`): a group trip shown on someone else's map ("Add to my map", docs/API.md "Group trips"). Nothing is copied: it counts in that map's `trips` (stats and `GET /maps`), never its photos. Completing the trip, or adding or changing a stop on a completed trip, marks `TRIP` visits on every linked map in the same transaction. Removing someone's `TripMember` row also removes the links they added, and so does removing their `MapMember` row from the trip's map unless they're on the trip itself (`deleteMapMember`, 17 Nov 2026); visits are never taken back. Map trip lists mark `linked`, `canOpen` and `organiserMapName`; someone who can't open a linked trip gets only its title, dates, status and countries.
- **`Moment`** (docs/API.md "Moments"): a life event or note on a map. `kind` is text checked against `utils/constants/momentKinds.js`; a title and optional text; `happenedOn` with `datePrecision` `DAY`/`MONTH`/`YEAR` (both null when undated, CHECK `moment_date_and_precision`); a country (required), a landmark in it or a place name; optionally a pin on its exact spot (`latitude`/`longitude`, and `spotShared`: whether viewers and share links see it, chosen per pin, off by default, the user's decision; CHECK `moment_spot`; rules in `utils/momentSpots.js`: in the moment's place with the coastline tolerance, `spotView` for who sees it, never rounded); optionally one of the map's own trips; `hidden`. `MomentTraveller` and `MomentPhoto` (with position, at most 20) link the people and photos. Access follows the photo rules (`momentRoleFor`, `canSeeMoment`, `canSeeMomentPhoto`, `momentPhotoScope` in `access.js`); dates and the opaque cursor are in `utils/moments.js`. `GET /maps/:mapId` gives `momentCountsByCountry` and `momentSpots` (the pins the caller may see: `findMomentSpots` in `domain/moments.js`). **Anniversaries** (21 Nov 2026, the user's wish; docs/API.md "Moments"): the kinds `FIRST_TRIP` and `ANNIVERSARY` (both lists, `MomentStamp`); `GET …/moments?anniversary=YYYY-MM-DD` (the viewer's today; `findAnniversaryMoments`, `utils/onThisDay.js` `anniversaryDay`) lists the moments dated to that day in earlier years, which the map shows as `components/moments/AnniversaryTag.jsx` (not on share links; closed for the day), and the "On this day" email sends one before any photo (`findOnThisDayMomentCandidates`, `pickAnniversary`, `LIFE_MOMENT_KINDS`; no picture when it has no photo). `tests/integration/familyExtras.test.js`, `tests/e2e-app/familyExtras.spec.js`.
- **Trips:**
  - **`Trip`**: status `PLANNED`, `ACTIVE` or `COMPLETED`. Completing a trip adds `TRIP` visits for its stops' countries. Every new trip has its `startDate` and `endDate` (23 Nov 2026, the user's wish: the "Welcome home" email below goes the day after it ends): `POST /maps/:mapId/trips` refuses one without both (400 `tripDatesRequired`), `PATCH` changes them but never clears them, `TripForm` asks for both, the mock the same (`dev/mock/tripDates.js`). The column stays nullable: imports, travel copies and older trips may have none. Integration tests that make a trip give it days.
  - **`TripStop`**: country, landmark or place name, dates and position.
  - **`TripTraveller`**: who went. Per-person stats come from trips.
  - **A trip's days and events** (23 Nov 2026, the user's wish: a safari day on a trip to Kenya): moments on a trip are its events; the kinds gained `SAFARI`, `HIKE`, `DIVE`, `TOUR`, `MEAL`, `SHOW`, `BEACH` (both `momentKinds.js`; the client's `TRIP_DAY_KINDS` and `TRIP_MOMENT_KIND_GROUPS`, offered first in a trip's moment form, `MomentStamp` icons). The trip page's Moments card lists them day by day (`utils/trips/tripDays.js` `tripMomentDays`: "Day 3 · Friday 4 July 2025", where the trip was from its stops, then "Other moments"; `MomentList` `groupMoments`, `data-moment-day`), once the trip has a start date. `tests/e2e-app/tripDays.spec.js`.
- **Photos:**
  - **`Photo`**: `latitude`/`longitude` are visible to editors only; `hidden` hides it from viewers. `kind` is `PHOTO` or `VIDEO` (First Class, docs/API.md "Videos"): a video lives in **Cloudflare Stream**, which converts it so every browser plays it: `streamUid`, `videoState` (`PROCESSING`/`READY`/`FAILED`), `videoBytes` (CHECK `photo_video_stream`), `durationS`, no position, no PhotoVariants; 20 per country apart from the photo cap; never a passport stamp; viewers see `READY` ones only (`canSeePhoto`, `photoListWhere`). Stream's API is `services/stream/streamApi.js` (the integration tests replace it with an in-memory Stream in `tests/integration/setup.js`), playback tokens and webhook checks are `utils/streamTokens.js` (signed locally with the account's signing key, no API call per view), and `services/stream/videos.js` keeps rows in step (the webhook `POST /api/videos/stream-webhook`, mounted raw before `express.json` like Stripe's; a refresh when an editor opens one still processing) and deletes Stream videos no row points at (the daily cron). `shotAt` is always `takenAt ?? createdAt` (set in `domain/photos.js` on create and when `takenAt` changes): the slideshows' order (`order=taken`) and the dated goals.
  - **`PhotoVariant`**: one per size, `THUMB`, `DISPLAY` or `ORIGINAL`, each a `MediaItem`.
  - **`PhotoLandmark`**: `GPS` suggestions with `confirmed: false`, or `MANUAL` matches. It carries the photo's `mapId` (a copy; a photo never changes map) and `confirmedAt` (null exactly while unconfirmed, CHECK `photo_landmark_confirmed_at`), so the passport and the landmark stat read this table alone (`[mapId, landmarkId]`). Every write sets both (`createPhotoInDB`, `upsertPhotoLandmarkInDB(photo, landmarkId)`, `confirmPhotoLandmarkInDB`).
- **The passport, badges and goals** (docs/API.md "Passport", "Badges and goals"; each traveller's own passport, 21 Nov 2026, the user's wish: `?traveller=` on both passport routes, `services/travel/passport.js` `travellerFacts` (their trips, the photos on them and in moments about them, those moments, their birth country; `findStamps`/`findFirstPhotoByCountry` take a `scope`), `map.traveller` on the reply; the page's "Whose passport" on a family's map, `BadgeOwnerSwitch` with `label`; the mock's `dev/mock/passport.js` the same):
  - a confirmed match is a passport **stamp**; `LandmarkCover` (map × landmark → a photo) is the photo an editor picked for it, pointing at the match itself (`[photoId, landmarkId]` → `PhotoLandmark`, cascade), so removing the match or the photo removes the choice. `services/travel/passport.js` builds the book (`domain/passport.js` `findStamps`: one `DISTINCT ON` query);
  - `BadgeAward` (map × badge key, `earnedAt`): the badges in `utils/badges.js` (regions in `utils/constants/regions.js`, copied by the client), awarded with `createMany … skipDuplicates` when the owner or an editor loads them, never taken back; World Heritage tiers 5, 25, 50, 100 and 250. **Country collections** (24 Nov 2026, the user's wish; docs/API.md "Badges and goals"): bronze, silver and gold for a quarter, half and all of a place's landmarks stamped (ticks too), keys `collect-<place>-<tier>` (`collectionTiers`, `collectionProgress`, `parseCollectionKey` in `utils/badges.js`; a tier needing as many as the next is left out, so one stamp never earns two), a place being a country or a UK nation on a split map, its total from `controllers/helpers/landmarks.js` `landmarkTotals(splitUk)` (all landmarks read once per instance); listed after the fixed badges for the places with a stamp or a tier awarded (`keepPlaces`), with `place` and `tier`; never per traveller;
  - `Goal` (`GoalKind` `COUNTRY_COUNT`/`COUNTRY_LIST`/`REGION`/`LANDMARK_COUNT`, with `target`, `countryCodes` or `region` as the kind needs: CHECK `goal_shape`; optional `deadline`, `achievedAt`), 50 per map; the rules in `utils/goals.js`, the counting in `services/travel/achievements.js` (`loadMapFacts`).
- **Monthly photo themes** (docs/API.md "Photo themes", Phase 2): `PhotoTheme` (one per `month`, stored as its first day, UTC; title, blurb), set ahead on `/admin/themes`, and `PhotoThemeEntry` (theme × photo, with the photo's `mapId`, `enteredById`, `enteredAt`): a map's editors enter its photos while the theme runs (photos only, 20 per map per theme, counted with the map row locked: `domain/themes.js` `enterPhotoInTheme`), take them out any time; the entries are the theme's album on that map, as its viewers see it (share links too). Members never see a future theme. Rules in `utils/themes.js`; `themes-3/6/12` badges count themes entered (`loadMapFacts` `themes`). The dev seed writes an example year (`prisma/seed/themes.js`).
- **Friends challenges** (docs/API.md "Friends challenges", Phase 2): `FriendChallenge` (title, `ChallengeMeasure` `NEW_COUNTRIES`/`COUNTRIES`/`LANDMARKS`/`THEME_PHOTOS`, `startsOn`/`endsOn` UTC days both included, at most 730 days apart, optional `target`, a unique `joinCode` made by `utils/shareCodes.js`, `createdById`) and `FriendChallengeEntry` (challenge × user, the `mapId` that counts: one they own or edit, null once the map is gone). ## Access and sharing

The full rules are in `docs/API.md`, under "Access":
- **Map role:** owner, else the `MapMember` role.
- **Trip role:** the higher of the map role and the `TripMember` role.
- **Link visitors:** a share code with link access on gives `VIEWER`, signed in or not, through `/api/shared/:code` only.
- **What viewers never get:** hidden photos, GPS coordinates or originals.
- **No role at all → 404** (so ids and codes can't be probed). A role that isn't enough → 403.
- **Adding or changing content needs a verified email** (`requireVerifiedEmail` in `middleware/auth.js`, put on each write route): countries, trips and stops, photos and landmark matches, new or deleted maps, every sharing change, avatars. Not needed: reads, setting up a map (`PATCH /maps/:mapId` and its travellers, which `/welcome` does straight after sign-up), map settings, the account, the contact form. It runs before any lookup, so it answers the same 403 for every id. A new write route needs it too. Integration tests' `signUp()` makes verified users unless given `{ verified: false }`.

- **The rules** are pure functions in `server/src/utils/access.js` (tested). Controllers load a row together with the caller's membership (`controllers/helpers/travel.js`: `loadMap`, `loadTrip`, `loadPhoto`, `loadUploadTarget`) and decide with them.
- **The Prisma trap these queries avoid:** `where: { userId: undefined }` means *no filter*. So the access loads in `domain/maps.js`, `domain/trips.js` and `domain/photos.js` only read the caller's `MapMember`/`TripMember` when there is a user. Keep that pattern in any new access query, or an anonymous request inherits the first member's role.
  - They read the row and the memberships as flat queries side by side (relation filters such as `map: { trips: { some: { id } } }`), not as nested selects, which Prisma runs one level after another; the result keeps the old shape (`members: []` or `[{ role }]`).
- **Trip-only editors** (a `TripMember` `EDITOR` with no editor role on the map) edit the trip, complete it and add photos to it; the TRIP and PHOTO visits that follow still go on the map. **Deleting a trip needs an editor role on the map, or owning it** (`canDeleteTrip`; the trip detail's `canDelete`).
- **Share codes:** 12 characters from `23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ`, shown `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`, normalised on lookup (`utils/shareCodes.js`). Reset = new code.
- **Invite tokens** are hashed with SHA-256.
- **The owner and co-owners:** the sharing routes (`controllers/sharing.js`, one module for maps and trips) and the map's settings; the owner alone deletes, transfers or picks the main map, and changes or removes a co-owner. Editors can't list members yet.
- **Adding people** (`addMember`) emails someone from our domain, so:
  - the owner's own address must be verified (403 otherwise, after the role checks), with a per-user daily cap on top of the per-IP one;
  - only an **active, verified** account becomes a member directly; anyone else (no account, unverified, deactivated) gets a `ShareInvite`, so an account squatting an address it never verified gets nothing;
  - the share email's subject is fixed; the sharer's name is only in the escaped body.
- **Taking your travels with you** (19 Nov 2026, the user's design; docs/API.md "Travel copies", FEATURE-PLAN.md): someone on a family map (any role) picks the trips they went on, the moments about them, countries' photos and photos one by one, says which traveller they are (never one linked to another account), and asks for copies on a map they own or co-own; the family map's owner or a co-owner approves or declines (`TravelCopyRequest`, `PENDING` → `APPROVED` → `DONE`, or `DECLINED`). Only the person asking starts it. The copies are the new map's own: R2 copies each file under the new map's keys (`copyPrivatePhotoFile` in `mediaService.js`; MediaItems recorded first, so an unsaved copy is the cron's), and each photo is saved through `createPhotoInDB` with the new map's plan caps (a photo that doesn't fit is skipped and counted), keeping its caption, date, position and landmark matches (passport stamps). Trips come with their stops (completed: `TRIP` visits), moments with their copied photos (spot private). Never hidden photos or moments, never videos (Stream). Every copy keeps `copiedFromId` (unique per map, no foreign key), so nothing is copied twice, by a later request either. Places follow the new map (`utils/travelCopies.js` `placeCopy`); first visits come from the copied photos and completed stops, only when earlier. `services/travel/travelCopies.js` `runTravelCopy` copies for 8 s a run, holding the request (`lockedUntil`) and saving `photoCursor` every 10 photos; approving runs the first, the browser on either side asks for more (`POST /travel-copies/:id/continue`, `hooks/useMapTravelCopies.js`, the review dialog), and the daily cron finishes any left (`continueApprovedCopies`). Emails (`users/travelCopyEmail.handlebars`): the request to the family map's owner, done or declined to the person asking. Client: `components/travelCopies/` (`TravelCopySection` in Settings "This map" for owners and co-owners, `TravelCopyDialog`, `TravelCopyPhotoPicker`, `TravelCopyReviewDialog`, `TravelCopyBanner` on the map), `api/travelCopiesApi.js`. Tested in `tests/travelCopies.test.js` and `tests/integration/travelCopies.test.js` (the fake bucket answers `CopyObjectCommand`).
- **Transferring a map:** `POST /maps/:mapId/transfer` (owner, verified; the new owner must be on the map, active and verified) and `POST /users/admin/transfer-map` (an admin, by email; never to the admin's own account, a developer's map only by a developer, and the new owner verified: 19 Nov 2026, the audit) share `transferMap` in `services/travel/maps.js`: one transaction, the grace rule above, and the old owner's default map recreated at once.
- **Leaving an account** (`services/travel/accounts.js`, from better-auth's `beforeDelete`):
  - first, a live subscription is cancelled with Stripe (`endSubscriptionOfDeletedAccount` in `services/billing/subscriptions.js`, called by `deleteUserMedia` in `auth.js`, for the person's delete and an admin's alike): the `Subscription` row cascades with the user, so without it Stripe went on charging a deleted account. If Stripe can't be told, the delete answers 503 and nothing is deleted; a subscription Stripe doesn't have counts as ended; with payments off nothing is sent;
  - the person chooses first (`GET /users/leaving-options`, `PUT /users/leaving-plan`, kept on `Profile.leavingPlan`): for each owned map, hand it to a member or delete it, and whether to leave their photos on other people's maps. A plan saved in the last 60 minutes is used; otherwise the defaults: each owned map with a co-owner or editor passes to the longest-standing active co-owner, else editor (a deactivated one only if no active one exists; `heirFor`, co-owners first since 19 Nov 2026), the others are deleted, and photos are left;
  - a handed-over map keeps every photo on it; photos on other people's maps are reassigned to that map's owner, or deleted with `leavePhotos: false`;
  - then the files of what was deleted, in batches (see Storage). Rows always go before files, so a failure leaves unused files for the cron, never a photo pointing at a missing file.
- **Accepting an invite** (`acceptInviteInDB` in `domain/sharing.js`, from `POST /invites/accept` and the sign-up/verify hooks) is one transaction that claims the invite first, so the same invite accepted twice counts once.

## The photo pipeline

1. **In the browser** (`client/src/utils/photos/`):
   - `readPhotoMeta.js` reads the date and GPS with `exifr`'s lite build (lazy-loaded; JPEG, not PNG);
   - `makePhotoCopies.js` draws `THUMB` (400 px) and `DISPLAY` (1600 px) on a canvas, which drops all EXIF. The original is kept untouched;
   - **no HEIC** (`docs/SITE-DESIGN.md`): an iPhone sends a JPEG when a photo is picked in a browser, so every size is JPEG, PNG or WebP, on the client, the server (`PHOTO_CONTENT_TYPES`) and the mock. A `.heic` file gets a message to save it as a JPEG.
2. **Upload** (`uploadPhoto.js` → `POST /api/maps/:mapId/photos/presign`): the browser `PUT`s each file straight to the **private** bucket (`putFile.js`, XHR for progress). Keys are `myplanetview/photos/<mapId>/<photoId>/<size>-<random>.<ext>`.
   - The presign takes each file's `contentType` and exact `bytes` (within `PHOTO_MAX_BYTES`), and the URL signs both (see Storage), so the `PUT` must send that type and a body of exactly that size. `putFile` refuses a blob whose size isn't the declared one.
   - A user may have at most 60 photo files presigned and not saved over the last 24 hours (`UNCONFIRMED_UPLOAD_LIMIT`); a presign above that answers 429.
3. **Confirm** (`POST /api/maps/:mapId/photos`):
   - only within 50 minutes of the presign (`PHOTO_SAVE_WINDOW_MINUTES`, below the cron's one-hour cutoff, so the cron never deletes a photo being saved);
   - the server HEADs each object (real size and type, within `PHOTO_MAX_BYTES`), then creates the `MediaItem`s, the `Photo` and its `PhotoVariant`s in one transaction;
   - with GPS, it sets the country (`utils/countryLookup.js`), adds a `PHOTO` visit and suggests landmarks within `matchRadiusM` (`utils/landmarkMatch.js`);
   - the client always sends the uploader's chosen `countryCode`: it's used without GPS, or when the GPS position is in no country (at sea);
   - a photo left with no country is listed by `GET …/photos?unplaced=1`, the map page's "Unplaced photos" (`components/map/UnplacedPhotosPanel.jsx`, opened from the stats and the country list), where an editor gives it a country.
4. **Viewing:** signed GETs (`signPhotoUrl`), signed at the start of the hour and valid for two (`PHOTO_URL_TTL_SECONDS`), so the browser cache works.
   - **Gallery order:** each photo has a `countryPosition` (its country's gallery on the map; the unplaced photos are one gallery) and a `tripPosition`, smallest first. A new photo, or one moved to another country or trip, goes first there. `POST /api/photos/:photoId/move` (`{ gallery: 'country' | 'trip', afterId }`) gives the photo the halfway point between its new neighbours (`utils/photoOrder.js`), renumbering the gallery only when there's no room. A country's order needs editor on the map, a trip's editor of the trip. In the client, `PhotoGrid`'s "Rearrange photos" (with `onMove`) swaps in `SortablePhotoGrid` (dnd-kit, lazy-loaded; mouse, long press on touch, keyboard, spoken steps); `usePhotoPage().movePhoto` moves the list at once, then saves, and reloads it if the save fails. The mock keeps the same order (`dev/mock/photoOrder.js`).
   - The `photo` entry in `MEDIA_CFG` is private, and the generic `/api/media-services` routes refuse its keys.
   - The daily cron never deletes media a `PhotoVariant` uses.
5. **Videos** (First Class; docs/API.md "Videos") skip R2: `utils/photos/uploadVideo.js` reads the length from the file's boxes (`videoFile.js`; the browser's reading, `videoElementLength.js`, when they don't say), overwrites the location (blanking XMP too; a file with XMP GPS at its top level, or a GPS track of its own, GoPro's `gpmd`, Google's `camm`, DJI's `djmd`, is refused: 19 Nov 2026, the audit), starts a Stream upload (`startVideoUpload`; the server refuses an upload address outside the CSP's hosts, naming it in the logged error, as no real upload has been seen yet: `UPLOAD_HOSTS` in `services/stream/streamApi.js`), sends the file there with tus-js-client in 50 MB pieces (`streamUpload.js`, lazy-loaded), then saves it with the ticket (`saveVideo`). Stream makes the thumbnails. A new video is `PROCESSING`: its tile says "Preparing…" (`PhotoThumbFace`), `usePhotoPage` asks about it again by itself, the viewer's `VideoPlayer` says it's being prepared (or couldn't be) with "Check now", and slideshows skip videos with nothing to play. Playback is `hooks/useVideoSource.js`: the browser's own HLS where it has it, else hls.js's light build, loaded only then; `data-playback` on the `<video>` says which. A video has no download: Stream keeps no original.

## The map: projection, shapes and country data

- **Projection.** The SVG world map is a **Robinson projection**, 2000 wide; the extent is the pole lines, y -16.6 to 1020.6 with Antarctica, -16.6 to 857 without (`mapExtent` in `projection.js`), measured against known coastlines (about ±2 units):
  - x = 987.1 + 5.681 × X(lat) × lng;
  - y = 502 − 518.6 × sign(lat) × Y(lat).
  - **Antarctica runs straight across the bottom** (16 Nov 2026, the user's wish: Robinson's curved sides made it follow an oval): from 58°S the parallels widen smoothly (`widened`, a smoothstep) until from 66°S the ±180° meridians are the map's sides (x 0 and 2000). North of 58°S it's plain Robinson. Only Antarctica's shapes moved (each vertex south of 58°S taken back to lat/lng with Robinson's exact inverse and forward again), the server's lookup entry for `AQ`, its label width, and the Magnetic South Pole's pin; GPS lookups stay consistent because the server projects with the same maths. A switch to Miller cylindrical (the whole map a rectangle) was tried the same day and rejected by the user as ugly: keep Robinson.

  It's implemented twice, in `client/src/utils/map/projection.js` and `server/src/utils/projection.js`, and the two must stay identical.
- **Shapes:**
  - `client/src/utils/map/countryShapes.js` is generated by `node images/src/tools/country-outlines.mjs <ne_10m_admin_0_map_units.geojson>` (from the repo root; 18 Nov 2026, the user's wish: the old outlines, traced from simplemaps.com's world SVG, were rough), keyed by ISO code, from Natural Earth's 1:10m admin 0 map units (public domain; on GitHub, nvkelso/natural-earth-vector, `geojson/`): projected with the map's own projection (unrounded), coasts simplified to 0.35 map units and land borders to 0.12 (a photo by a border lands on the right side: Maseru is Lesotho's), each shared border simplified once so neighbours always meet; islands under 1 unit left out unless their place is small (those get 0.06 and two decimals, so Tuvalu or the Maldives keep a shape) or another place's land is within 6 units (Symi and Leros, off Turkey). ## Client architecture

- **Routing.**
  - `client/src/App.jsx` is the route table; path constants live in `utils/constants/routes.js`. Never hardcode paths.
  - Home and FAQ are eager. Every other page is a `lazyPage()` in `utils/navigation/lazyPages.js`, with its static path in `PAGES_BY_PATH`, used for the first-render preload and Navbar prefetch.
  - `hooks/FocusMainOnRouteChange.js` (used by `App`) focuses `#main-content` after a route change and scrolls to the top, or, when the address has a `#hash`, to that element once the (lazy) page has drawn it. Back and forward return to where that history entry was scrolled (noted per `location.key` while scrolling), once the page is tall enough (17 Nov 2026, the audit: they landed at the top; `tests/e2e-app/navigation.spec.js`). It's the one place hash links are handled: pages don't scroll to their own hash.
- **Adding a page touches:**
  - `App.jsx` and `routes.js`;
  - if it's public, `PUBLIC_STATIC_ROUTES` plus `utils/data/helmetData.js` and `metaData.js`;
  - if it's browser-only (signed in, share or invite links), `CLIENT_ONLY_ROUTE_PREFIXES`, or the hosts will 404 it;
  - and `lazyPages.js`.
- **Guards** (`utils/user/AuthenticateUser.jsx`):
  - `AuthenticateUser`: `/map`, `/maps/:mapId`, `/trips/:tripId`, `/welcome`, `/account`, `/challenges`, `/challenges/:challengeId`, `/gifts`, `/import`, `/compare`;
  - `AuthenticateAdmin` (ADMIN or DEVELOPER): `/admin`, `/admin/contact-forms`, `/admin/users`, `/admin/promo-codes`, `/admin/offers`, `/admin/themes`, `/admin/feedback`;
  - `AuthenticateDeveloper`: `/admin/events`;
  - no guard: `/shared/:code`, `/embed/:code`, `/guess/:code`, `/invite/:token`, `/challenges/join/:code`, `/gift/:code` and `/unsubscribe`.

  The guards pass `state.from`. Client checks are UX only; the server enforces everything.
- **Analytics** (GA4, `utils/analytics/`) starts only after cookie consent, on idle; `index.html` has no Google preconnects, so nothing contacts Google before that.
  - **Secret addresses:** `ANALYTICS_EXCLUDED_PREFIXES` (`/shared`, `/challenges/join`, `/gift`, `/unsubscribe`, `/invite`, `/reset-password`, `/verify-email-address`). GA is never first loaded on one; once loaded, `window['ga-disable-<ID>']` is set while one shows and cleared after (`trackAddress` in `initGA.js`, run on every route change and from a `history` hook installed before gtag.js, so it is set before gtag.js could report the change). Web vitals (`utils/seo/vitals.js`) aren't reported there.
  - **Redaction** (`privacy.js`): every `page_location` and `page_referrer` GA gets is the route pattern (`/shared/:code`, `/maps/:mapId`; `ANALYTICS_REDACTED_ROUTES`) with only `utm_*` parameters and no hash; another site's referrer is cut to its origin. App sends the page views itself (`send_page_view: false`). `page_title` is the same route pattern, never `document.title`, which on the map, trip and passport pages is the person's map or trip name (`initGA.js` `titleOf`; 19 Nov 2026, the audit).
  - A new route with a secret or an id in its path goes in those two lists in `routes.js`. Paths are matched in lower case (the router ignores case; 19 Nov 2026, the audit).
  - Don't replace `window.gtag` with a wrapper that calls `ReactGA.gtag`: that calls `window.gtag` back, and the loop swallowed every event.
  - **Withdrawing consent:** the footer's "Cookie settings" (Legal column, `openCookieSettings()` in `consent.js`) makes App show the banner at once, saying what was chosen, with focus on "No thanks"; Escape or its close stamp shuts it unchanged. A no (now or from an earlier visit) calls `stopGA` (`initGA.js`): `ga-disable-<ID>` stays set whatever the page, GA's consent becomes denied, queued page views are dropped, and the `_ga`, `_ga_*`, `_gid` and `_gat` cookies are deleted on this host and its parent domains. A yes again in the same visit resumes the GA already loaded.
  - **Web vitals** (`utils/seo/vitals.js`, with `web-vitals`) load only once GA runs (`initGA.js`), and report only while `analyticsActive()`.
- **Installable app (PWA)** (docs/FEATURE-PLAN.md "Installable app"; data online only): `manifest.json` from `vite.config.js` (with `id`), and a hand-written service worker, `scripts/sw-template.js`, which the `siteServiceWorker` plugin writes to `dist/sw.js` at build with a build id (from the bundle's file names: a new deploy is a new worker, and old caches go), `CLIENT_ONLY_ROUTE_PREFIXES`, the public pages (`PUBLIC_PAGES`: the pre-render's list) and every script, stylesheet and font of the build (`SITE_CODE`, about 1 MB; not the `online-*` chunks, hls.js, tus-js-client and the pre-render's server renderer, which need the network or are never loaded: 19 Nov 2026). It caches the app shell and the files it names, every `/assets/` file it's asked for (cache first), and public pages (network first); offline, a browser-only or secret page opens the app shell, a public page its last copy (any query ignored). **The installed app keeps the whole public site** (the user's decision, 16 Nov 2026): when the page runs standalone (`display-mode: standalone`, or `navigator.standalone` on iPhone) and the device isn't saving data, `registerServiceWorker.js` posts `keep-site`, and the worker fetches whatever of `SITE_CODE` and `PUBLIC_PAGES` this build hasn't kept, so every public page opens offline; a browser tab keeps only what it visits. Kept copies are matched with `ignoreVary` (`BY_ADDRESS`): a host answering `Vary: Origin` (vite preview does) made every kept script miss offline, as the page's requests carry an Origin the worker's don't. It never touches the API, another site (signed photo and video links, posters) or a POST, and never stores a browser-only page's HTML. `utils/pwa/registerServiceWorker.js` registers it from `main.jsx` in production builds only, after load, never in the pre-render or a browser driven by tests (`navigator.webdriver`). `components/utils/OfflineNotice.jsx` (in App) says "You're offline" while `navigator.onLine` is false (a store with an online server snapshot, so hydration matches), and `AppErrorScreen` says so too when a page's code couldn't download offline, reloading once back online. `sw.js` is served `no-cache` (`.htaccess`, `netlify.toml`). The icons are the brand's (`brand-assets.mjs`), with a maskable 512 px one. Checked on a verify build with a headless smoke test (controlled, offline `/map` opens the shell, nothing private cached; 16 Nov: as the installed app, all 27 public pages kept and FAQ, pricing, about, the countries index and a guide opened offline unvisited, a tab keeping none; the test overrides `navigator.webdriver` and `matchMedia` so the site registers the worker itself); `tests/e2e-app/offline.spec.js` covers the notice.
- **Providers:** `main.jsx` mounts `ErrorBoundary` (`utils/error/`) → `HeadProvider` → `BrowserRouter` → `AuthProvider` → `App`. A render error, or a lazy page whose code didn't download, shows `components/utils/AppErrorScreen.jsx` (a paper card on the poster frame with a reload button); it sits outside the router and auth, so it uses plain links.
  - **Hydration:** a pre-rendered page (`data-prerendered` on `<html>`, a filled `#root`) is hydrated with `hydrateRoot`; the app shell is drawn with `createRoot`. 404.html is drawn from scratch when the address is a real page in another case (`/About`). The first render must be the same in the browser as at build: nothing in a render may read storage, the window size, the date or `Math.random`; such things go in effects (or `useSyncExternalStore` with a server snapshot, as the footer's year does with `__BUILD_YEAR__` from `vite.config.js`). Auth starts signed out and the cookie banner closed for the same reason.
- **Auth:** a cookie session (`withCredentials`); `useAuth()` exposes better-auth's account actions. The session request (`auth/bootSession.js`) goes out from `main.jsx` at boot, alongside the first page's code, when this browser has the `isLoggedIn` flag; `AuthProvider` takes it in its first effect. Logging out clears the state only once the server has answered (or a 401 says the session had already ended); otherwise the nav or account page says they're still logged in. Only the server's answer ends a session (a 401, or a reply with no user): any other failed read (offline, a server error, a timeout, at boot too) changes nothing and keeps the `isLoggedIn` flag, so the next load asks again (17 Nov 2026, the audit: a failed boot read used to sign the browser out for good). While the user is unverified, the session is read again when the tab is focused or shown (at most every 10 s), so verifying in another tab lifts this tab's locks; the verify page reads it straight after verifying. Session reads are numbered, and an older answer never replaces a newer one. The account page's avatar upload is locked for an unverified user ("Verify your email to add a photo."); removing stays allowed. Sign-up goes to `/welcome`, the onboarding that edits the default map. The login, sign-up, forgot and reset pages share `components/auth/AuthPageLayout` (red poster frame, one `<main>`) and `TravelTicket` (a boarding-pass card: parchment header with the page's h1, the form on theme tokens, a decorative `TicketStub`).
- **Mock mode** (development only; the default for `npm run dev`, as the user asked to be always signed in as a test user):
  - `main.jsx` imports `src/dev/mock/installMock.js` when `import.meta.env.DEV` and `devUsesMock()`. That is true when MODE is `mock`, or unless the browser chose the real API: `?api=real` / `?api=mock` are stored as `localStorage['mpv:apiSource']`, and the badge has "Use real API".
  - Production builds compile `DEV` to false, so none of it ships (checked: no mock strings in `dist/`, including `mpv:apiSource`).
  - It puts an Axios adapter (`mockAdapter.js`) on the exported `httpClient` and on the global axios, and patches XHR for the presigned-upload host (`mockXhr.js`), so no request leaves the browser. Like R2, the mock bucket refuses (403) a `PUT` whose `Content-Type` or size isn't the presigned one (`dev/mock/uploads.js`).
  - The handlers in `src/dev/mock/handlers/` follow `docs/API.md` and the access rules. The store (`store.js`, seeded by `seed.js`) lives in localStorage; uploaded files are session blob URLs. A browser keeps its saved store, so a `seed.js` change that an old store would hide (the real test photos, 16 Nov 2026) bumps `STORE_KEY` (now `mpvMockStore.v2`; `map.spec.js` reads it too).

## Server architecture

- **`src/server.js`** wires the global middleware in this order:
  1. helmet (CSP off);
  2. `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` and `Cache-Control: no-store` on everything (a route that may be cached sets its own: countries and landmarks, offers, themes);
  3. request context (`X-Request-Id`);
  4. morgan (`utils/requestLog.js`: its `url` token is redacted, see Logging);
  5. CORS from `FRONTEND_ORIGIN` (comma-separated; also better-auth's trusted origins);
  6. `originCheck` (`middleware/originCheck.js`, CSRF): a `POST`/`PUT`/`PATCH`/`DELETE` whose `Origin` isn't in `FRONTEND_ORIGIN`, or with no `Origin` and `Sec-Fetch-Site: cross-site`, gets 403. CORS alone only drops the headers; the request would still run. No `Origin` and no `Sec-Fetch-Site` passes (curl, supertest);
  7. JSON parsing (2000 kb). **No `express.urlencoded`**: a cross-site HTML form can only send urlencoded, multipart or text/plain;
  8. `globalRateLimiter` (in memory, 1000 per 15 min per IP or IPv6 /64; only a backstop);
  9. better-auth at `/api/auth/*`.

  Then the routers: `/api/events`, `/api/users` (including `/map-settings`, `/export` ("Download my data": `controllers/dataExport.js`, `domain/dataExport.js`, the file's shape in `utils/dataExport.js`) and the admin's `/admin/promo-codes`), `/api/contact`, `/api/media-services`, `/api` (`/countries`, `/landmarks`), `/api/maps` (including `/:mapId/passport`, `/badges`, `/goals`), `/api/trips`, `/api/photos`, `/api/moments`, `/api/goals`, `/api/promo-codes` (the public check), `/api/gifts` (gifts of a year of a plan: yours, a gift's page by its code, redeeming; buying is `/api/billing/gift-checkout`), `/api/emails` ("On this day"'s daily run and the links in its emails), `/api/offers` (the live travel offers), `/api/themes` (this month's photo theme and the recent ones), `/api/challenges` (friends challenges; `/join/:code` for anyone), `/api/feedback` (a report from the map and its screenshot's presign; the admin's are `/api/users/admin/feedback`), `/go` (an offer card's link: counts the click and redirects), `/api/shared`, `/api/invites`, `/api/billing`. `trust proxy` is 1 (Vercel).
- **Per feature:**
  - `routes/` attaches middleware;
  - `controllers/` sanitises input (`utils/sanitize.js`, `utils/travelValidation.js`), calls domain functions, logs events and replies;
  - `domain/` holds thin Prisma queries. **Never a `_count` in a select:** Prisma joins a `GROUP BY` over the whole child table, so every list counted every user's rows. List counts (`counts` on `GET /maps`, `stopCount`/`photoCount` on trip lists) come from `domain/counts.js`: a `groupBy` over the ids already loaded (`WHERE "mapId" IN (…) GROUP BY`), run in parallel and merged in. Hidden photos count only where the caller is the owner or an editor: `findOwnedMaps` always, `findMemberMaps`/`findTripsSharedWithUser` per membership role, `findTripsForMap(mapId, { includeHidden })` from the caller (default false);
  - `event/<x>Utils.js` builds events (`travelUtils.js` for the travel features);
  - pure rules go in `utils/<x>.js` with a test.
- **Responses:**
  - `sendDataResponse(res, code, payload)` → `{ status, data }`; `sendMessageResponse(res, code, message)` → `{ status, message }`, which is what the client shows;
  - use `DOMAINS`/`EVENT_MESSAGES` (`utils/responses/responses.js`);
  - failures are error event classes (`event/errorUtils.js`);
  - travel controllers wrap handlers in `handler()` from `controllers/helpers/travel.js`.
- **Logging:** `logEvent(...)` writes `AppEvent` without awaiting, for writes only (never reads), through `runAfterResponse(promise)` (`utils/background.js`, Vercel `waitUntil`, failures logged and swallowed). Use `runAfterResponse` for anything else after the response, emails especially.
  - **No secrets or addresses in logs:** `AppEvent.path` and the morgan line go through `utils/redactUrl.js` (share codes, challenge join codes and promo codes → `:code`, invite and better-auth tokens → `:token`, query values other than plain filters → `:redacted`). Events never hold an email address: people are `actorId`/`targetId`, and an address with no account is `emailRef(email)` (`utils/emailRef.js`, a short HMAC). Keep it that way in new events.
  - Only 5xx events keep a stack (`createGenericErrorEvent`, and `createAppEvent` drops it for any 4xx).
  - `get-all-events` returns the newest 500 (`ALL_EVENTS_LIMIT`); the admin page uses the paged route.
  - `deleteOldEvents({ olderThanDays })` in `domain/events.js` is the 90-day retention, called by the daily cron.
- **Database connections** (`src/prisma.js`): one `pg` Pool per function instance behind the Prisma adapter, `max` from `DB_POOL_MAX` (default 5), `connectionTimeoutMillis` and `idleTimeoutMillis` of 5000, and `attachDatabasePool(pool)` from `@vercel/functions`, which keeps a Vercel instance alive until idle connections close (it does nothing elsewhere).
  - In production `DATABASE_URL` must be the provider's **pooled**, transaction-mode URL. The adapter sends unnamed statements, which such poolers accept.
  - `DIRECT_DATABASE_URL` is the direct URL; only the Prisma CLI uses it (`prisma.config.ts`).
- **Rate limits** (all values in `utils/constants/rateLimitConstants.js`; every limiter is off under test unless `RATE_LIMITS_IN_TESTS=true`, which only `tests/integration/rateLimits.test.js` sets):
  - in memory (`middleware/rateLimit.js`): only the global backstop;
  - database-backed (`middleware/dbRateLimit.js`, the `RateLimit` table, shared across instances): share-code lookups 30 (opening a link, plus any `/shared/:code/…` request whose code matches nothing, `middleware/shareLookup.js`), browsing a link 300, invite accepts 30, promo code checks 30, gift lookups and redeeming 30 (per IP), an email's links 120 (per IP), an import's photo presigns 600 per user (a presign carrying `importedFrom`, instead of the 120 below) and its trip batches 60 per user, offer clicks counted 60 (past it `/go` still redirects, uncounted), contact form 5, generic uploads 20 (presign and confirm together), photo presigns 120 (all per 15 min), adding people 20 an hour per IP and 25 a day per user, new maps 20 a day per user (`keyFor: keyByUserId`);
  - the count is one atomic `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE` (a passed window restarts at 1 in the same statement); IPs are keyed by `clientIpKey` (IPv4, or an IPv6 /64, via express-rate-limit's `ipKeyGenerator`);
  - better-auth's own limiter is on the database too (`rateLimit.storage: 'database'`, model `AuthRateLimit`), keyed on `x-forwarded-for` (Vercel overwrites it with the visitor's address; a header with several values isn't trusted) and the path, IPv6 per /64.
- **Auth** (`src/auth.js`, better-auth 1.7.20 with the Prisma adapter):
  - role and `isActive` live on the DB user and are declared as `user.additionalFields` (`input: false`: a sign-up gets the defaults, `update-user` refuses them). The cookie cache is off, so `getSession` reads the session and its user from the database on every request, and `validateAuthentication`/`attachUserIfPresent` use that user without a second query: a role change or a deactivation applies to the next request;
  - a deactivated user can't create sessions;
  - ids are UUIDs, as every other table's (17 Nov 2026, the user's wish): `advanced.database.generateId: 'uuid'`, so on Postgres better-auth sends no id and the `@default(uuid())` on `User`, `Session`, `Account`, `Verification` and `AuthRateLimit` fills it in (Prisma's, so no migration). Rows made before then keep better-auth's own random ids;
  - user `create.after` makes the default map, and accepts pending invites if the address is already verified (since better-auth 1.5, after hooks run once the user's transaction has committed);
  - user `update.after` accepts invites once the email is verified (a promo code's free month waits for the first paid Checkout);
  - `deleteUser.beforeDelete` releases the travel data (see above), then avatars.
  - `hooks.before`: `/delete-user` needs `password` (better-auth then checks it; without one it accepts any session younger than `freshAge`, a day. 1.4.13 multiplied `freshAge` by 1000 twice; fixed in 1.6, but the hook stays); `/sign-up/email` and `/update-user` clean `name` (`utils/displayName.js`: one line, no control characters, 80 at most), since names go into share emails; and a new password (`/sign-up/email`, `/reset-password`, `/change-password`) must follow the client's rules (`utils/passwordRules.js`, the same pattern as `client/src/utils/regex/regex.js`; keep the two in step), 400 otherwise. The password check only applies over HTTP, so the seed's `auth.api` sign-ups keep `12345678`. `/sign-up/email` may carry a `promoCode` (better-auth ignores fields it doesn't know): the before hook refuses one that can't be used (400, before the account exists), and the after hook records it on the new profile (`recordSignUpPromoCode`).
  - `changeEmail.sendChangeEmailConfirmation` (still the 1.7 name; the old alias `sendChangeEmailVerification` was removed in 1.5): a verified account confirms from its current address first (`users/userChangeEmailConfirmation.handlebars`, client link with `?change=confirm`), then better-auth's `/verify-email` sends the usual verification to the new address. An unverified account goes straight to the new address. An address that already has an account gets the same 200 and no email.
  - Cookies are `sameSite: 'none'`, `secure` and `__Secure-` in production (`defaultCookieAttributes`, so every cookie better-auth sets); keep `useSecureCookies` tied to `isProd` (`tests/authConfig.test.js` checks the production settings). **Switch to `sameSite: 'lax'` once the API is on `api.<domain>`** (same site as the client); `originCheck` guards writes meanwhile.
- **Storage** (`services/s3/`):
  - `s3public.js` (`STORAGE_*`): avatars and contact images;
  - `s3private.js` (`STORAGE_PRIVATE_*`, each falling back to its `STORAGE_*` twin): photos;
  - keys are prefixed with `BucketNamespace` (`myplanetview`);
  - `mediaService.js` loads the AWS SDK and both bucket modules on its first bucket call (`storage()`), and `emailHandler.js` loads `resend` and `handlebars` on the first email: a cold start that touches neither skips them (about 90 ms locally). Keep other code from importing `s3public.js`, `s3private.js` or the SDK directly;
  - both clients set `requestChecksumCalculation`/`responseChecksumValidation: 'WHEN_REQUIRED'`. Without it, AWS SDK 3.729+ signs an empty-body CRC32 into every presigned PUT URL, so browser uploads fail. DeleteObjects still sends `x-amz-checksum-crc32` (the operation requires a checksum; `tests/mediaBatches.test.js`), which R2 has accepted since February 2025; that needs one real test. The buckets are in R2's EU jurisdiction, so the endpoint is `https://<account-id>.eu.r2.cloudflarestorage.com`;
  - **Deleting files** (`deleteStoredObjects`): one `DeleteObjectsCommand` per 1,000 keys of a bucket, then the MediaItem rows of the files that went (never a row a photo or an avatar still uses); if a whole request fails, single deletes, eight at a time. A file that can't be deleted keeps its row for the cron. Delete the rows that point at files (PhotoVariant, via the photo or map) first, then the files;
  - **Every presigned PUT signs `Content-Type` and `Content-Length`** (`services/s3/signedUpload.js`; `X-Amz-SignedHeaders=content-length;content-type;host`, checked offline in `tests/signedUpload.test.js`). The presigner leaves `content-type` unsigned unless it's in `signableHeaders`; `content-length` is signed once `ContentLength` is set; `CacheControl` on a presigned PUT does nothing (never signed or hoisted). That R2 enforces the signed length still needs one real test (a PUT of another size should get 403).
  - `MEDIA_CFG` in `mediaService.js` sets each resource's visibility, exact `allowedTypes` and `maxBytes`. The generic uploads (avatars, contact images) take PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF up to 5 MB; the key's extension comes from a MIME map (`utils/media/uploadRules.js`), never from the file name. No SVG anywhere: it can carry scripts.
  - Generic uploads: presign needs `bytes`; `/confirm-upload` HEADs the object and deletes it on a type or size mismatch (the row stays unconfirmed for the cron). Only confirmed uploads can be used: an avatar must be the user's own, of an allowed type; a contact form's `imageKey` must also be under an hour old and on no other form. Contact uploads need no account (the public contact form). A signed-in user may have at most 20 unconfirmed generic uploads over the last 24 hours (429 above).
  - **The daily Vercel cron** (`/api/media-services/cron/cleanup-orphans`, `CRON_SECRET`; `cleanupOrphanUploadsHandler` in `controllers/mediaGeneric.js`):
    1. purges, together and each on its own (a failure is logged and reported as `null`): finished rate-limit windows, `AppEvent` rows older than 90 days, expired sessions and verifications, invites accepted or expired over 30 days ago, and gifts whose Checkout was never finished, after 2 days;
    2. then deletes unused uploads (`deleteOrphanUploads`): `findOrphanUploads` (`domain/mediaGeneric.js`, one raw query) leaves out files a PhotoVariant, an avatar or a map design uses and contact images a form names, and takes photo files after an hour and the rest after a day, oldest first, 500 a batch. Each batch is one DeleteObjects per bucket and one row delete; it moves past files it couldn't delete, and starts no batch after 40 s (`CRON_BUDGET_MS`; `vercel.json` gives the function 60 s).
- **Billing** (Stripe; `routes/billing.js`, `controllers/billing.js`, `services/billing/`): `GET /api/billing`; checkout, change-plan, extra-maps and portal need a verified email and are rate limited per user, and answer 503 without `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`. The Stripe SDK loads lazily (`services/billing/stripe.js`), price ids come from the `STRIPE_PRICE_*` keys. The webhook `POST /api/billing/webhook` is mounted in `server.js` with `express.raw` **before** `express.json`: it checks the signature, records the event id first (a repeat answers 200 and does nothing; a failure removes the record and answers 500, so Stripe retries), and handles `checkout.session.completed` and `customer.subscription.*`, fetching each subscription from Stripe as it is now (`latestSubscription`: events can come late or out of order) and cancelling a second live one for the same account (`isDuplicateSubscription`, logged for a refund). Checkout keeps one Stripe customer per account and expires its other open sessions; a new plan during a promo free month ends it, and extra maps wait until it has ended (17 Nov 2026, the audit). Admin grants: `/api/users/admin/plan-grants/:userId` and `/api/users/admin/end-plan-grant/:grantId`.
- **Email** (Resend, `services/email/emailHandler.js`): renders the handlebars templates in `users/` and `contact/` and sends them with the `resend` SDK from `EMAIL_FROM`, using `RESEND_API_KEY`.
  - Profiles: `userAdmin` (share invites, `users/shareEmail.handlebars`), `userVerify`, `userReset`, `userChangeEmail` (`users/userChangeEmailConfirmation.handlebars`, to the current address) and `contact` (reply-to is the person who sent the form).
  - Network, rate-limit and 5xx failures retry twice under one idempotency key; quota and validation errors don't.
  - In production it throws at import without both keys; locally it logs each email and its link instead of sending.
  - Until a domain is verified in Resend, `EMAIL_FROM=onboarding@resend.dev` only delivers to the Resend account's own address.

## Known gaps

- **Untested for real:** the API is tested end to end against Postgres (`npm run test:integration`), but nothing has run against real R2 (the bucket client is mocked there), real Cloudflare Stream (an in-memory Stream there, and the mock's in the browser) or in a real browser on the live site. Uploads, signed URLs and the client flows need trying for real, and so does the travel copies' server-side copy (`CopyObjectCommand` with `CopySource`; R2 supports it, but it has only met the fake bucket).
- **The API through Netlify** (17 Nov 2026, while there's no domain): called at `my-planet-view.vercel.app` from the Netlify site, the API's cookies were third-party, which Safari and many phone browsers never keep, so signing in on a phone ended in "No valid session found". The Netlify site now passes `/api/*` and `/go/*` through to the API (the first rules `scripts/prerender.mjs` writes into `_redirects`, `API_PROXY_TARGET`; the prerender asks the API itself for those paths), and `VITE_API_URL` must be the Netlify site's own address (set in Netlify's environment variables), so the browser only talks to the site and the cookie is its own. Every API reply is `Cache-Control: no-store` unless its route sets its own, so Netlify's CDN never keeps one person's reply for another. While proxied, Vercel sees Netlify's address as every visitor's, so the per-IP rate limits (and better-auth's) are shared by everyone: fine for a test, not for launch. Once the API is on `api.<domain>`, drop the rules and switch the cookies to `lax`. Apache (`.htaccess`, InMotion) has no such rule.
- **Placeholders:**
  - the domain;
  - the CSP's bucket and Stream hosts (whole Cloudflare domains for now) and its API host (`my-planet-view.vercel.app`).
- **Data:** border photos can land in the neighbouring country. The coarse lookup folds Jersey, Guernsey and the Isle of Man into the UK (so on a split map they go to the nearest nation), and puts Dundalk (Ireland) in the drawn Northern Ireland.
- **Passport:** the zero-length round-capped pins were checked in Chromium only (Safari to try).
- **Sharing:** editors can't see who else has access.
- **Account enumeration:** sign-up still answers 422 for an address that has an account. better-auth only hides it with `requireEmailVerification: true` (no session until the address is verified), which would change the sign-up → `/welcome` flow; the user decided to leave it as it is (19 Nov 2026; `docs/AUDIT-2026-11-19.md`).
- **Unused code:** a few client files are kept on purpose although nothing imports them: spare UI inputs and `deleteMediaObject`.
- **Tests:** the travel pages' Playwright tests (`tests/e2e-app/`) run with reduced motion (the animated sea isn't exercised) and in mock mode only, not against the real API, R2 or a production build. They find controls by role and name and count the seeded demo data, so a renamed control or a changed `seed.js` means updating them.
- **Plans:** no Pricing page feature is marked `soon` (First Class's "extra map styles" were taken off on 21 Nov 2026, when every map got its own design, free); a new one marked so shows as "Coming soon" until it's built. The payment terms are drafted (`termsData.js` 1.1.0, 16 Nov 2026: plans and prices, renewing and cancelling, "14 days to change your mind" (a full refund within 14 days of a first payment, the user's choice; `REFUND_DAYS` in `utils/plans/billing.js`, also on the Pricing page), changing plan, free memberships and promo codes; Stripe in the privacy policy): the user, or someone qualified, reviews them before payments go live.
- **Analytics:** turn GA4's "page changes based on browser history events" off. The app sends its own redacted page views; GA's history ones would double them and could carry a secret address as the referrer.
