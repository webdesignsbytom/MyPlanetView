# Audit, 26 Nov 2026

A site-wide check before going live, a week after the last one (`docs/AUDIT-2026-11-19.md`). Most of what it covers was built since then: map designs and styles, gifts, imports, "On this day" and "Welcome home" emails, referrals, strings, guess games, flights, Life's One Shots, the visited look, shared slideshows, passport photos and covers, feedback, Vs mode, the supporter badge and the embed.

This file is the working checklist: tick an item when its fix is in and verified, and add a note if the fix differs from what's written here. Effort: **S** about an hour or less, **M** a few hours, **L** more.

**How it was checked**
- **Automated:** every check CI runs, a dependency audit, a verify build's chunk sizes, and the migrations diffed against `schema.prisma` on a throwaway PGlite database.
- **The contract:** a script compared the server's 245 routes with the mock's handlers, the mock's verified-email list and `docs/API.md`.
- **In the browser:** every page (43 addresses, signed in and out, admin, share, embed, guess, show, join, gift and error pages) opened in mock mode at 1440×900 and 390×844, recording console errors, failed requests, h1 and main counts, sideways scrolling and broken images, with an accessibility scan (axe, WCAG 2.2 AA) and a screenshot of each. Then the map's load and its main panels timed, and zooming in checked.
- **Reading the code:** the routes anyone can call, rate limits behind the Netlify proxy, the email crons, account deletion's clean-up, the CSP, robots, sitemap and head tags, the admin pages, the privacy policy and pricing claims against the features.
- **What couldn't be checked:** real Stripe, R2, Stream and Resend; the live hosts; Lighthouse and the full production build (both read env files: CI runs the build and it passes); Vercel's project settings (they need a team sign-in).
