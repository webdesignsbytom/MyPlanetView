# Statue of St. Michael and dragon (sourced model)

- Model: "Statue of St. Michael and dragon", https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/statue-of-st-michael-and-dragon-7a4d3e18bf1b41c2ad9dc8fe20855a4a
- Author: CUNO jiannibang, https://sketchfab.com/jiannibang
- Licence: CC BY 4.0, http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- A reconstruction from drone footage of a real gilded statue of the archangel standing on the
  dragon, on a spire's top (the author's page links to it on statues.vanderkrogt.net, record
  frbn060).
- Downloaded 26 Nov 2026 as glb (st-michael.glb), unchanged.
- Used in ../../grand-place/ (gilded on top of the Town Hall's spire, standing in for Brussels' own
  St Michael slaying the dragon), ../../cordoba-mezquita/ (gilded, standing in for St Raphael on the
  bell tower), ../../angel-of-independence/ (cut from the dragon and gilded, standing in for the
  winged Victory on the column) and ../../castel-sant-angelo/ (cut from the dragon: in bronze for the
  archangel on the castle's top, and in white marble, small, for the angels on the Ponte
  Sant'Angelo's parapets). Credit it wherever the model's picture is shown.
