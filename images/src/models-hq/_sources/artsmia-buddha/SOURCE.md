- Model: "Enshrined Buddha, c. 1850 CE", https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/enshrined-buddha-c-1850-ce-e6a846a1ab9149d3a0614c7b82bcc8eb
- Author: Minneapolis Institute of Art (artsmia), https://sketchfab.com/artsmia
- Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- A scan of a seated Burmese Buddha of about 1850 in the museum's collection.
- Downloaded 26 Nov 2026 as glb (artsmia-buddha.glb), unchanged.
- Used in ../../leshan-buddha/: cut from its base, in the cliff's red sandstone, standing in for the
  Leshan Giant Buddha (which sits with its feet down and hands on its knees; no open scan of it
  was found: the one model on Sketchfab named for it is a wooded hillside with no figure). No credit
  is required; it's kept here all the same.
