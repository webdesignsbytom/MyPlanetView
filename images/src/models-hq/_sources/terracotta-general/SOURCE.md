# Terracotta Army general (sourced model)

- Model: "Terrakotta Armee - General", https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/terrakotta-armee-general-7f688e465ed341b5867501dbfff9f6c4
- Author: funix, https://sketchfab.com/funix
- Licence: CC BY 4.0, http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- A scan of a full-size replica of a Type I general with his hands crossed before him, from the
  exhibition "Die Terrakotta Armee" in Lübeck (2025).
- Downloaded 26 Nov 2026 as glb (terracotta-general.glb), unchanged. `terracotta-general-light.glb` is a
  light copy made from it by `make-light.py` (its parts joined, seams merged, decimated to about
  25,000 faces, no texture), which the diorama reads: the full scan, copied 400 times, would not render.
- Used in ../../terracotta-army/: cut light and stood in clay in every place of Pit 1, each a
  little turned and sized, standing in for the army's many kinds of warrior. Credit it wherever the
  model's picture is shown.
