# Pegasus, Palacio de Bellas Artes (sourced model)

- Model: "Escultura de Pegasus (II)", https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/escultura-de-pegasus-ii-8a8f48625f7749869a73d8b5326811b4
- Author: miguelbandera, https://sketchfab.com/miguelbandera
- Licence: CC BY 4.0, http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- A photogrammetry model of one of Agustí Querol's Pegasus statues in front of the Palacio de
  Bellas Artes, made from the Open Heritage 3D dataset (DOI 10.26301/v1pz-0r22).
- Downloaded 26 Nov 2026 as glb (pegasus.glb), unchanged.
- Used in ../../bellas-artes/: cut from its pedestal, in bronze, as the four Pegasus statues on the
  esplanade. Credit it wherever the model's picture is shown.
